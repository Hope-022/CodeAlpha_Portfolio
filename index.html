About Me

From words to Interfaces

I'm Chibudom Idenyi, a frontend engineer with a genuine drive for building good, well-crafted websites.


My path here started with a Bachelor's degree in English and Literary Studies. My curiosity about tech began after graduation, when I noticed something simple but important: a well-designed website lets anyone navigate it and get what they came for, without needing help — even on a site they've never used before. That observation is what pulled me toward frontend engineering.


Today, I build interfaces with that same goal in mind: seamless experiences that work for anyone, regardless of age or how tech-savvy they are.


I'm also an on-air personality at a radio station, and through that experience I've built strong communication skills — expressing ideas clearly, researching thoroughly, and working well within a team. Those same instincts now shape how I approach every interface I build: clearly, deliberately, and with the user always in mind.
